    About

    MedCost is a rebuilt version of Medical-Insurance-Cost-Predictor. What changed: duplicates removed before splitting; honest cross-validated model selection; a smoker × obesity feature; a gradient-boosting model with a robust (Huber) loss; 90% prediction ranges calibrated separately for smokers and non-smokers; what-if scenarios; warnings outside the training range; sex and region dropped from pricing.

    Educational tool trained on a small public US dataset (1,337 people, ages 18–64). Not a quote, not financial or medical advice.
